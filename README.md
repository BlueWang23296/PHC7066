# PHC 7066 Class Notes

Professor Datta does not provide his own lecture notes for PHC7066. This set of notes was created to serve as a comprehensive resource for students taking the course. The notes cover not only the material discussed in class, but also a number of prerequisite concepts that Professor Datta may not explicitly review during lectures but assumes students are already familiar with. 

Also an exercise book including all problems in past homework, exams and QE is included. They may serve as practices when preparing for exams.

I have invested a considerable amount of time in sorting these notes, but I cannot guarantee that they are completely free of errors. If you notice any mistakes, unclear explanations, missing material, or typos, please feel free to contact me at **[wwang4@ufl.edu](mailto:wwang4@ufl.edu)**. You will be acknowledged in the **Contributors** list.
